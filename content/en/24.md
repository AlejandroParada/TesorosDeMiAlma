# 24 Maybe I'll Have to Go Home

"And whatsoever they shall speak when moved upon by the Holy Ghost shall be scripture, shall be the will of the Lord, shall be the mind of the Lord, shall be the word of the Lord, shall be the voice of the Lord, and the power of God unto salvation"

During my mission I had some personal questions and prayed fervently asking for answers—only God knew my thoughts. Later I got sick with hepatitis while serving in Parral. A clinical exam came back positive for Hepatitis. My fear was that maybe they would send me home, but I felt a peace in my heart to stay calm. The first thing they gave me was a health blessing. I had to go to Concepción (regional capital) and stayed at the mission president's home for about 4 days while waiting for lab results. I was healed miraculously fast enough to return to the mission field: the lab results came back completely clean! to the amazement of the doctors, because clinically it was impossible given the high values I had.

During those days, the mission president received us in his home; each night we read the scriptures together with him and his wife. On one occasion, the president felt inspired to speak to my companion and me personally. His words reached me deeply. I felt it was the Lord himself answering my personal questions, word by word, exactly what I needed. Only God knew what was in my heart.

That day I knew I heard His voice. And I still hear it today.
