# Perhaps I Will Have to Go Home

> **“For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the Lord. For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts.”** (Isaiah 55:8–9)

> **“And whatsoever they shall speak when moved upon by the Holy Ghost shall be scripture, shall be the will of the Lord, shall be the mind of the Lord, shall be the word of the Lord, shall be the voice of the Lord, and the power of God unto salvation.”** (Doctrine and Covenants 68:4)

## Unanswered Questions

During my mission, I went through a period of deep personal questions that constantly occupied my thoughts. I prayed fervently asking for answers from heaven, knowing that only God knew the anguish and the questions I kept in my heart. Day after day, I lifted my petitions to the Lord, trusting that He would answer according to His time and wisdom.

## An Unexpected Trial

While serving in the city of Parral, something unexpected happened: I fell ill with hepatitis. The symptoms appeared gradually, but when I finally underwent a clinical examination, the results were clear and concerning: positive for hepatitis.

My first thought was one of fear. “Perhaps they will send me back home,” I thought, feeling that my mission might come to a premature end. Yet in the midst of that uncertainty, I experienced something extraordinary: a deep and inexplicable peace filled my heart, as if a voice whispered “be still.”

## The Power of Priesthood Blessings

The first thing I received was a blessing of healing administered by my priesthood leaders. In that sacred moment, I felt the healing power of God manifest through His authorized servants. The faith of those around me combined with mine, creating an atmosphere of hope and trust in divine power.

## Days of Waiting at the Mission President’s Home

I had to travel to Concepción, the regional capital, where I remained at the mission president’s home for four days while waiting for the definitive laboratory results. These were days of intense reflection, where faith and hope mingled with natural human concern.

## A Medical Miracle

The results that arrived were absolutely extraordinary: completely clean! The doctors were astonished because, clinically, it was impossible to explain this recovery considering the high values I had initially shown. What for medical science was inexplicable, for me was a clear manifestation of the Lord’s healing power.

I was healed miraculously quickly enough to return to the mission field, confirming that God had a specific purpose for me to continue serving.

## Nights of Scripture and Revelation

During those days at the mission president’s home, each night we gathered to read the scriptures with him and his wife. These study sessions became deep spiritual experiences, where we felt the presence of the Spirit in a tangible way.

## The Lord’s Voice Through His Servant

On one memorable occasion, the president felt inspired to address my companion and me personally. When he began to speak, his words penetrated directly into my soul. Every phrase, every counsel, every teaching seemed designed specifically to answer the questions I had been carrying in my heart for weeks.

It was as if the Lord Himself were answering my personal questions, word by word, exactly what I needed to hear. Only God knew the depth of my thoughts and concerns, and through His authorized servant, He provided the answers I had so sought in prayer.

## Recognizing His Voice

In that sacred moment, I knew with absolute certainty that I was hearing the voice of the Lord. It was not simply the well-intentioned counsel of a leader; it was divine revelation flowing through someone who had been called and set apart to be an instrument in the hands of God.

## A Promise That Endures

That day marked my soul forever. I learned to recognize the voice of the Lord when it comes through His authorized servants and when it comes directly to my heart through the Holy Ghost.

## Present Reflection

Years have passed since that experience, but its impact remains vivid in my memory and in my testimony. And the most beautiful thing is that I still hear His voice today. The same Spirit that guided me during my mission continues to be my constant companion, whispering truths to my soul and guiding me along the path of righteousness.

The illness I feared might end my mission became a blessing that strengthened my faith for a lifetime. Sometimes what we perceive as obstacles are in reality opportunities that God uses to teach and bless us in ways we could never imagine.
