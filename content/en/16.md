# A Visit to Marcelo at the Home

> **"And now, as ye are desirous to come into the fold of God, and to be called his people... are willing to mourn with those that mourn; yea, and comfort those that stand in need of comfort."** ([Mosiah 18:8–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/18?lang=eng&id=p8-p9#p8))

> **"For it shall be given you in the very hour, yea, in the very moment, what ye shall say."** ([Doctrine and Covenants 84:85](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/84?lang=eng&id=p85#p85))

*Note: In this account, the names have been changed; the real names are not used.*

## A friend still fighting to rise

I have a friend who lives in a residential home because of struggles related to addiction. In the past he was shot and left in a wheelchair. In spite of everything, he has managed many of his difficulties with courage, though he still fights day by day.

I thought I might help him focus and overcome some of the unease that still accompanied him. It occurred to me to give him a crafts kit: pliers, wire, and simple tools so he could shape something with his hands, create, concentrate, and feel useful.

## The impression I could no longer postpone

I bought the kit to take to him, but for one reason or another I kept putting off the visit. One day I felt a clear impression that I should go see Marcelo. I did not heed it.

The next day that impression grew stronger, until it became very intense. I even felt: "Go today, go today, do not delay, go today." It was almost as if I were being shaken inside to make me obey.

At last I obeyed and went to the home.

## I arrived just in time

The woman who ran the home received me with relief: "Thank goodness you came. Marcelo tried to harm himself, but we were able to stop him in time. Now you have arrived just in time!"

I stayed with Marcelo. We talked. I listened. I helped him as best I could. I also gave him the crafts kit, which helped him make a few things with his hands, focus better, and have a concrete reason to work.

I told him that whatever he made we would give to others in need, in that home or in other homes. That idea gave him motivation: not only to create for himself, but also to bless others.

Afterwards, the woman told me that what I had done would help contain him and lift him out of his discouragement.

## What I learned from that visit

I learned that the Holy Ghost knows the exact moment. When we feel an insistent impression to go, to call, or to show up, sometimes we do not understand why… until we arrive.

I also learned that to minister does not always require great speeches. Sometimes it is arriving, listening, accompanying, and placing in someone's hands a simple tool—and with it, a reason to keep going.

That visit became a blessing for Marcelo, for those who cared for him, and for me. It reminded me that the Gospel of Jesus Christ is lived when we respond in time, with love, and with a willingness to be present when we are needed most.
