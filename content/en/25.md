# Be Still, and Know That I Am God

> **“Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.”** ([Psalm 46:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ps/46?lang=eng&id=p10#p10))

> **“And they are as the angels of God, and… can show themselves unto whatsoever person it seemeth them good.”** ([3 Nephi 28:30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/28?lang=eng&id=p30#p30))

## A Testimony That Transcends Time

During my mission in Chile, I heard from the lips of a missionary an extraordinary account that had occurred a year before my arrival. This young elder had been an eyewitness of an event that deeply marked his faith and that of all who were present that day.

## The Zone Conference in Concepción

The event took place in the city of Concepción, Chile, during a zone conference. These meetings are a common practice in missions to provide instruction from mission leaders and spiritually strengthen the Lord’s servants.

Between two and three zones had gathered in a chapel (possibly the Talcahuano stake center, according to what that missionary remembered), where both elders and sister missionaries participated.

## The Moment of Terror

At a certain moment, the unthinkable happened: the chapel was stormed by an armed group that took everyone present as hostages. It was something that completely took the mission leaders by surprise and that sowed terror in the hearts of those young servants of the Lord.

## The Appearance of the Mysterious Protector

In those moments of extreme danger, according to the missionary witness, something extraordinary occurred. A person wearing “a gray overcoat” appeared among them. He had the appearance of just another missionary: short hair, youthful look, seeming another “tall gringo” like so many other servants of the Lord.

Yet no one remembered having seen him before during their mission. It was as if he had appeared out of nowhere.

## Words of Peace Amid Chaos

This mysterious visitor stood in front of the group of missionaries and, with serene but firm authority, told them to “calm down and that everything would be all right.” He spoke from the front, set apart from the group, as if he were a choir director guiding his congregation.

His words had an immediate and supernatural effect: everyone calmed down and grew quiet. An inexplicable peace filled that chapel which moments earlier had been full of terror.

## Invisible to the Aggressors

The most extraordinary part of the account was that the armed men seemed unable to see this heavenly protector. If they had seen him, they surely would have acted with unfortunate consequence at such boldness in standing before them without any fear.

The members of the armed group passed near him, but simply did not notice him. It was as if he were completely invisible to their eyes, yet perfectly visible and real to the missionaries who needed his help.

## A Miraculous Change of Heart

After a few tense moments, the leader of the armed group suddenly ordered his men that they must withdraw because “things had not turned out as they expected.” They withdrew peacefully, without causing any harm to the missionaries.

This drastic change of attitude was considered by all as “a miracle” given the seriousness of their initial intentions. The missionaries watched carefully as the armed group withdrew, observing how they left the building just as they had arrived, but now with changed hearts.

## The Disappearance of the Angel

Once spirits had settled, the missionaries made the corresponding report and the police arrived to take statements. But when they looked for the “gringo in the gray overcoat” to include him in the testimonies, they discovered something astonishing: no one saw him leave.

He had appeared as an angel to protect them and had disappeared in the same mysterious way. Everyone agreed that he had come as a heavenly messenger sent by the Lord to be their protector in that moment of extreme danger.

## The Witness’s Testimony

I had the invaluable blessing of hearing this account directly from a missionary who had been an eyewitness. He was a young man who had only recently arrived in his mission, and that had been his first zone conference. His testimony was pure and powerful as he testified of the Lord’s mercy in protecting them that day.

## Reflections on Heavenly Beings

This account has led me to reflect deeply on the scriptures that speak of heavenly beings who can manifest themselves to help the children of God. In [3 Nephi 28:30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/28?lang=eng&id=p30#p30) we read of the three Nephites: **“And they are as the angels of God, and… can show themselves unto whatsoever person it seemeth them good.”**

I am astonished at the similarity between this account and the biblical descriptions of angelic interventions. It reaffirms my testimony that there is a divine pattern for recognizing these manifestations, that there exists an order and purpose in all things of the Lord.

## A Personal Connection

Sometimes I have wondered whether it might have been some loved one who had passed to the other side who was with them that day. I do not know with certainty, but the scriptures teach us that those who have departed can be instruments in the Lord’s hands to bless and protect His children.

## The Promise of Greater Light

This experience reaffirms a fundamental truth: if we live in harmony with the restored Gospel, we will have more spiritual light, greater divine protection, and an increased sensitivity to recognize the manifestations of heaven.

Let us remember the Savior’s words: **“Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you”** ([Matthew 7:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/7?lang=eng&id=p7#p7)). When we live worthily, the Lord will provide the help we need, sometimes in ways that go beyond our mortal understanding.

## The Reality of Divine Protection

This testimony has strengthened my conviction that the Lord watches over His servants in ways we often do not fully understand. Missionaries who serve faithfully are under His special care, and He will send the help they need, whether through natural or supernatural means.

“Be still, and know that I am God”—these words of the psalmist take on deep meaning when we remember that our Heavenly Father has the power to calm any storm, protect any faithful servant, and manifest His love through heavenly ministers when need requires it.
