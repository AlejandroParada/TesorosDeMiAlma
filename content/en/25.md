25 Be Still and Know That I Am God...

"...they shall not be known... they are as the angels of God... they can show themselves unto whosoever... it seemeth them good" (3 Nephi 28:26-30)

At that time I heard from the mouth of a missionary who was present at an event one year before I arrived on my mission in the city of Concepción, Chile. There were 2 or 3 zones gathered in a chapel (possibly the Talcahuano stake center at that time—remember that boundaries can change over time) to have a zone conference (usual practice in missions to provide instruction from mission leaders), where Elders and Sister missionaries participate.

At a certain moment the chapel was assaulted by an armed group that took them hostage, something that took the mission leaders by surprise. In those moments, the missionary told me that a person wearing "a gray overcoat," who looked like another missionary, with short hair, seemed like another "tall gringo," but whom no one remembered seeing before during their mission, stood in front of them and told them to "calm down and that everything would turn out well." He spoke to them in front and separate from the group, as if he were a choir director, and everyone calmed down or became tranquil. It seems that those with weapons couldn't see him, otherwise they would have acted with unfortunate consequences for this person given such audacity. Those from the armed group passed near him, but they didn't notice him. After a moment their leader ordered them that they should withdraw because things didn't turn out as they expected—they withdrew peacefully. This change of attitude was considered "a miracle" due to the gravity of their intentions. Everyone was very attentive to the armed group's withdrawal and that they left the building just as they came. After they calmed down, they made the report and police came to take statements. What everyone remembers is that the "gringo in the gray overcoat" was never seen again, or no one saw him leave—only that he came like an "angel" to protect them. I had the blessing of hearing it from a missionary who was a witness, who had just arrived on his mission and was at his first zone conference, testifying of the Lord's mercy in protecting them that day.

I have wondered if it wasn't Shubert the principal who was with them—I don't know, but his features are the same. "...they shall not be known... they are as the angels of God... they can show themselves unto whosoever... it seemeth them good" (3 Nephi 28:26-30). I am amazed by the similarity, and it reaffirms that there is a pattern to recognize, that there is order and purpose in all things.

If we live in harmony with the restored Gospel, we will have more light. Let us remember "ask and ye shall receive, knock and it shall be opened unto you."
