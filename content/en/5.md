# 5 The Other Roast

*“For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in.”* — [Matthew 25:35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/25?lang=eng&id=p35#p35)

It was the eve of May 1st, and everyone was in the usual routine of shopping to spend Labor Day with a traditional family barbecue. Although I did not feel entirely well enough to celebrate, it was something we had already planned as a family. The day before I had been dismissed from my job, so I decided to look ahead without allowing sadness and discouragement to overwhelm me completely.

I was shopping at the supermarket, specifically in the butcher section, and was about to order the meat for the roast when I clearly felt a spiritual impression: “Buy another roast.”

“All right, I’ll do it,” I said to myself, and I bought another roast without questioning the inspiration.

As I left the store, I reflected: “I bought two roasts—what else should I do?” And then another clear thought came to my mind: “Do you remember that family of limited means in the ward? They surely won’t have a roast on May 1st. Well, you take them the roast.”

The impression was so clear that I could not ignore it. That same night I went to their house with “the surprise.” When I knocked on the door and handed them the roast, the family had no words to thank me. Their faces lit up in a way I will never forget.

“I know it isn’t very large, but you’ll all be able to eat at home,” I told them sincerely.

For them, that day was not very different from the others. A family of limited economic means can rarely imagine seeing a roast on their table, especially on a special date. But that day would be completely different for them: it would be a day of greater family unity, because a shared meal is always a precious occasion to draw a family closer together.

What I did not know at that moment was that, by giving in the midst of my own financial difficulty, I was learning one of life’s most important lessons: when we serve others, especially when we ourselves are going through trials, our own burdens are lightened.

That day I understood that impressions of the Spirit come especially when we are willing to act in faith, even when our own circumstances are not ideal. Sometimes the best way to overcome our own challenges is to serve those who are in greater need than we are.

True joy does not come from what we have, but from what we share.
