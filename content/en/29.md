# 29 Dad Tied a Tie for a Stranger on the Street

> **"Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares."** ([Hebrews 13:2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/13?lang=eng&id=p2#p2))

> **"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."** ([Matthew 5:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/5?lang=eng&id=p16#p16))

## A hurried afternoon on the way to the meeting

One weekday afternoon we were leaving as a family to attend a Church meeting. The clock showed that it was already late if we wanted to arrive on time, so we were all in a hurry. My father walked with a quick step, and we followed, trying to keep his pace, aware that every minute counted if we wanted to get there in time.

## The unexpected encounter

When we had barely gone half a block from home, a tall young stranger stopped us. He was wearing an elegant suit and shirt, and he was accompanied by a young couple who were also dressed formally. It was clear they were heading to some special event: a wedding.

The young man came straight up to my father, who was also wearing a suit and tie, as was his custom for Church meetings, and with a certain urgency but respectfully said: "Sir, sir, please, could you tie my tie? I don't know how to do it."

## The decision of a moment

In that precise instant, I observed something extraordinary in my father. His attitude changed completely. He slowed his hurried step, stopped altogether, and with a kind smile answered: "All right."

## The act of service

My father took the young man's tie with care and patience. He could have made a simple, quick knot just to get it over with, but that is not what he did. He tied the knot with the same dedication and care he used when he arranged his own tie, adjusting the length and the height perfectly so that it would look right on the young man.

## Sincere gratitude

The young man was deeply grateful for this simple but meaningful act. His face showed relief and genuine gratitude. Now he could attend that wedding feeling properly dressed and confident.

## The lesson that marked my soul

I was completely astonished by the transformation I witnessed in my father. In a matter of seconds, his attitude changed from "let's hurry, we're late" to "someone needs help, and I can give it." There was no hesitation, no annoyance at the interruption. There was only a heart willing to serve.

## A silent testimony

This experience was engraved deeply on my heart. I sincerely believe that my father's action, that silent testimony of Christian love, was engraved on my soul more deeply than many of the words spoken in the Church meeting that day.

## The true gospel in action

Years later, I understand that on that afternoon I witnessed the gospel of Jesus Christ in action. My father did not preach a sermon on service, nor did he quote scriptures about loving one's neighbor. He simply lived these principles.

## Lessons from a simple moment

### **Service has no schedule**
Opportunities to serve do not always come at convenient times. They often appear when we are busy, in a hurry, or focused on our own plans. True Christian character is revealed in how we respond to these divine interruptions.

### **Small acts have great impact**
Tying a necktie may seem insignificant, but for that young man it meant the difference between arriving ashamed or confident at an important occasion. Small acts of kindness often have repercussions beyond what we can imagine.

### **Example teaches more than words**
Children constantly watch their parents. In that moment, my father did not know he was giving one of the most powerful lessons of my life. His spontaneous example of Christian charity became a treasure for my soul.

### **The light of Christ shines through everyday actions**
We do not need to be in a chapel or in a formal setting for our light to shine. Streets, casual encounters, unexpected moments can become opportunities to show the love of God.

## The lasting impact

This experience literally became a **treasure for my soul**. It taught me that the gospel is not only something we study or talk about on Sundays; it is something we live every day, in every encounter, with every person who crosses our path.

## Ministering as Christ ministered

My father, without knowing it, was following the Savior's perfect example. Christ also stopped to minister to individuals, no matter how busy He was or how many crowds were waiting for Him. He saw individual needs and responded with love and compassion.

## A prayer of gratitude

When I reflect on this memory, my heart fills with gratitude to my father for teaching me, through his example, what it means to have a heart willing to serve. His action that day planted a seed in my heart that has continued to grow through the years.

## An invitation for everyone

This experience invites me to ask myself: Am I willing to stop my plans when someone needs help? Does my light shine enough for others to see it and come near when they need help? Am I creating **treasures for the soul** in the lives of my own children through my everyday actions?

## Scriptures that come to life

That day, the scriptures came to life before my eyes. I saw the Savior's words fulfilled: **"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven"** ([Matthew 5:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/5?lang=eng&id=p16#p16)).

## Final reflection

Sometimes the simplest moments become the deepest lessons. A tie knotted with love, a heart willing to serve, a silent but powerful example: these are the true treasures that endure in the soul.

My father taught me that the gospel is lived best when it is expressed in spontaneous acts of kindness toward strangers who need our help. These moments of Christian charity become eternal treasures that enrich not only the one who receives the service, but also the one who gives it and all who have the blessing of witnessing it.
