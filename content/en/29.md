29 Dad Tied a Tie for a Strange Man on the Street

We were going out as a family one weekday afternoon for a church meeting—it was late to arrive on time to the meeting, so we were in a hurry. When we were half a block from home, a tall young stranger stopped us, wearing a suit and shirt. He was accompanied by a young couple also dressed formally. They were going to a wedding, and the first young man said to my father (who was wearing a suit and tie), "Sir, sir, please could you tie my tie? I don't know how to do it."

My father stopped and slowed his hurried pace, and said, "Alright." He took the tie and tied the knot. He could have made a simple knot, but he did it as if for himself and with the right length for the young man's height. The young man was very grateful for his action. I was amazed by my father's change of attitude from "let's go fast, we're going to be late" to "someone needs help and I can give it to him." I believe his action remained in my soul much more than what was discussed in the meeting—it became a treasure for my soul.
