# 8 The Blessing That Night in the Hospital

*“Bring them hither… for I have compassion upon you…”* — 3 Nephi 17:7

Some years ago, during a cold winter, I was asked to accompany an acquaintance from the ward to give a blessing of healing at the Pereira Rossell Hospital. I was wearing a large, long coat that protected me from the night cold.

A dear friend asked if I could go at night to give a blessing to her mother, who had suffered a stroke. Her mother was not a member of the Church, but her daughter’s faith motivated her to ask for this spiritual help.

When we arrived at the hospital near 10 at night, something extraordinary began to happen. Upon explaining that we were going to give a blessing of healing, the doors literally opened before us. This was truly miraculous, since at that hour the front desk and security rarely allow visitors in. Three doors and security posts opened without major difficulty.

Arriving at the gynecology ward, we found a room with many beds. The main lights of the room had already been turned off, creating an atmosphere of quiet and rest. We located the woman’s bed, gently woke her, and explained that her daughter had asked us to come give her a blessing of healing.

She turned on a small lamp beside her bed, and with great patience we explained how a blessing works: through faith in Jesus Christ and the power of the priesthood. With reverence, we proceeded to give her the blessing. Afterward we spoke briefly with her.

I had taken a few steps back, away from the side of the bed, when suddenly I felt as if my coat had caught on something. Looking behind me, I discovered that the woman in the neighboring bed had gently tugged my coat.

In a shy but hopeful voice, she asked me: “Could I receive a blessing too?”

My companion and I looked at each other, moved by her request. We explained again how the blessing “worked” and proceeded to give it to her with the same love and reverence. Immediately afterward, another patient nearby also asked for a blessing.

I do not remember exactly how many blessings we gave that sacred night, but in one of them I experienced something I will never be able to forget. During a blessing, I clearly felt that my Heavenly Friend placed His hands upon mine. The most extraordinary thing was that it was my acquaintance who was pronouncing the blessing, and I had my hands on his, but I felt other, different hands on top of ours.

There are not enough words that can describe that moment so sacred and special! I believe the veil between heaven and earth grew very thin that night, as I had never perceived it before. They were not just someone else’s hands; there was something different in His palms—yes, right in the middle… it felt so clear and real.

I know that Christ lives and has been very near me. I feel it when I encourage those who are saddest, those who are alone, those who seem to matter to no one. When I am doing the work of God, He reaffirms my testimony in tangible and spiritual ways.

Sometimes I understand that the trials that come to me are to see what I do with what I know. And what I know, I will never deny. Though everything be set against me, I will not deny what I felt that night. I prefer that when the Lord returns He find me doing His work, that He see that my heart is full of love for others.

I only let Him guide me, warn me, and even shake me if necessary. Because I know that He lives, and that His love extends to all His children, regardless of their beliefs or circumstances.
