# 8 The Blessing That Night at the Hospital

"Bring them here... for I have compassion upon you..." (3 Nephi 17:7)

Once I had to go with an acquaintance from the neighborhood to give a health blessing at Pereira Rosell Hospital. This was several years ago—it was winter and I was wearing a big, long coat. A friend asked me if I could go at night to give a blessing to her mother who had suffered a stroke (cerebrovascular accident). Her mother was not a member of the Church. When we went, it was about 10 at night, and when we said we were going to give a health blessing, doors began opening (where rarely do the doorkeeper and security let people in at that hour). That was truly a miracle—three doors or security checkpoints opened. When we arrived it was a room with many beds (it was the gynecology ward). They had already turned off the lights in the room. We located the bed and woke up the sister, and we explained that her daughter had asked us to give her a health blessing. She turned on a lamp where we were. We explained that a blessing worked through faith in Jesus Christ. Then we gave her the blessing. Afterward we talked a little. I had taken a few steps back to the side of the bed, then I felt as if my coat had gotten caught on something. When I looked back, I saw that the lady in the other bed had pulled my coat and said: "Could I receive a blessing too?..." We looked at each other with the other acquaintance and then we explained how the blessing "worked." We proceeded to give her the blessing, and again the one next to her also asked for a blessing... I don't remember how many blessings we gave that night, but on one occasion, during a blessing, I felt like my Friend had put his hands on top of mine. What was most peculiar was that it was my acquaintance who was giving the blessing and I had my hands on his...

There are not enough words to describe that special moment! I believe the veil became very thin that night like I had never perceived before. They weren't just someone else's hands—there was something different in their palms, right in the middle... it felt so clear! I know that Christ lives—he has been very close to me. I feel it when I encourage the saddest, those who are alone, those who matter to no one. If I am doing God's work, he reaffirms my testimony. Sometimes I understand the trials that come my way—it's to see what I do with what I know. And what I know I will not deny. Even if everything turns against me, I will not deny what I felt. I prefer that when the Lord returns, he finds me doing his work, that he sees my heart is full of love for others. I just let Him guide me and warn me, and even shake me if necessary!
