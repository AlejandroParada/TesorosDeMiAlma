# The Counsel I Need

> **“For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.”** (Hebrews 4:12)

> **“…the preaching of the word had a great tendency to lead the people to do that which was just… it had had more powerful effect upon the minds of the people than the sword, or anything else, which had happened unto them.”** (Alma 31:5)

## An Unexpected Request

Last week, a coworker approached me with a request that took me by surprise. With a sincere and humble attitude, he asked if I could give him some counsel about a personal difference he had with another colleague. His words moved me deeply: he told me he felt trust in sharing his concern with me and that he valued my perspective.

## The Art of Listening

Recognizing the trust he had placed in me, I tried to offer him words of comfort and, more importantly, to listen to him with genuine attention. During our conversation, I could perceive how gradually his emotional burden was lightened. There was something liberating in the simple act of being heard without judgment, with understanding and empathy.

## The Savior’s Words

In that moment, I remembered the comforting words of the Savior that resonate in perfect harmony for these situations:

> *“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.”* (Matthew 11:28–30)

## The Transforming Power of the Word

This experience confirmed to me once again the transforming power that the word of God possesses when it is shared with love and sincerity. As the prophet Alma teaches us, the word has “a great tendency to lead the people to do that which was just” and can exert “a more powerful effect upon the minds” than any other earthly influence.

## Ministering Everywhere

I firmly believe in the importance of ministering in every place and to every person, regardless of context or circumstances. This experience reminded me that we must always be spiritually prepared, because we never know when someone will need our counsel, our understanding, or simply our attentive ear.

## The Light Others Perceive

When we live the principles of the Gospel with authenticity and allow the light of Christ to shine through us, others perceive it naturally. We do not need to force opportunities to share our faith; they present themselves organically when we cultivate a life of righteousness and compassion.

## Personal Reflection

This experience taught me that often we are instruments in the Lord’s hands without even fully realizing it. When we are spiritually prepared and live according to Christian principles, we become beacons of hope for those around us.

## Constant Preparation

The testimony we possess and the light we radiate do not go unnoticed. Others see it, feel it, and, when they are ready, seek to draw near to it. That is why it is so important to maintain constant spiritual preparation through prayer, scripture study, and obedience to the commandments.

We never know when we will be called to be the counsel someone desperately needs to hear.
