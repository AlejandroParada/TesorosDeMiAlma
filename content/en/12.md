# I Arrived Without a Chain on My Mission

> **"And I will go before your face. I will be on your right hand and on your left, and my Spirit shall be in your hearts, and mine angels round about you, to bear you up."** ([Doctrine and Covenants 84:88](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/84?lang=eng&id=p88#p88))

> **"Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths."** ([Proverbs 3:5–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/prov/3?lang=eng&id=p5-p6#p5))

## Teaching in Santa Bárbara

On one occasion during my mission, we taught in a town called Santa Bárbara, south of the city of Los Ángeles, in Chile. Proselyting there was not easy: the people were not always receptive, and the conduct of some previous missionaries had not been right, which had left marks we had to overcome with patience and example.

## A referral in the mountains

We received a referral that came from the mountains: a family living toward the Andes range, in the countryside, about twelve kilometers from Santa Bárbara.

At first we went by bus, but there were very few departures. Later we managed to borrow bicycles from the town to make the trip, which we repeated two or three times a week.

The road had steep hills, dirt stretches, and sections of highway. It was not an easy ride: it was effort, dust, climbs, and the determination not to abandon that family.

## Precarious bikes and a prayer of faith

On one occasion the bicycles were in very poor condition—truly precarious. Even so, we decided to go to the appointment. We entrusted ourselves to God and asked Him to protect us on the way.

We went, we taught, and we fulfilled our duty. The Lord accompanied us on that difficult journey.

## The miracle of the return

On the way back in the afternoon, when we were about to return the bicycles, I noticed something extraordinary: mine had lost its chain. My companion’s had lost its brake.

We had covered kilometers of hills and uncertain roads… and yet we arrived. We were protected by the Lord in a miraculous way. We did not fully understand it at the time, but we clearly felt that His hand had been with us.

## What I learned without a chain

I learned that sometimes the Lord allows us to keep going even when, humanly speaking, it seems we lack what is essential. I arrived “without a chain,” but not without faith. My companion arrived without a brake, but not without protection.

That experience was engraved on my soul as a quiet testimony: when we strive to fulfill a mission with a sincere heart, God can supply what is missing and sustain us in ways we only later recognize as miracles.

Ever since then, whenever I remember those battered bicycles and that road toward the mountains, I also remember that the Gospel of Jesus Christ is preached not only with words, but with tired feet, with effort, and with the certainty that He goes before our face.
