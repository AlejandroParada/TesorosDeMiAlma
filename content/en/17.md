# Dad's Visit (in Dreams)

> **"And it shall come to pass afterward, that I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions."** ([Joel 2:28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/joel/2?lang=eng&id=p28#p28))

> **"For for this cause was the gospel preached also to them that are dead, that they might be judged according to men in the flesh, but live according to God in the spirit."** ([1 Peter 4:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-pet/4?lang=eng&id=p6#p6))

## The first dream: a meeting in the Temple

My father passed away in 2003. On the Friday of Easter week in 2004, I had a dream that deeply marked my soul.

I dreamed that we were going to the Temple and that I met my father by the recommend desk. The arrangement of the desk was the reverse of what was usual; that is, it was on the right side. He greeted me warmly and said: "How good to see you here. I am so glad. I hope you come more often."

When I crossed paths with him, I had not realized that he was no longer alive. Only after walking a few meters did I suddenly understand. I said to myself: "But Dad had passed away." Then I turned back, and I no longer saw him there.

I approached a Temple worker who was nearby and asked if I could see the list of the brethren who had been there that day. I began to review the names one by one until I clearly felt that I should stop at one in particular. I did not know him, but the Holy Ghost whispered to me that, in the spirit world, he was now known by that name.

## The second dream: a sacred farewell

The following year, on the same date—and without my consciously remembering what had happened—I dreamed again. I was walking along a dirt road when my father came toward me.

This time I was able to realize and be aware that he no longer lived on this earth. I said to him: "Hi, Dad. Are you still working in the Temple?"

He answered that he was not, and that on that occasion he had come to say goodbye because he had been sent to serve a mission in a distant place, and he had been given permission to come and take his leave. Then the dream ended.

## What I learned about the other side

From these experiences I learned that life continues after this mortal existence. I learned that the true Church of Jesus Christ is organized and established on the other side as well, just as it is here on earth: there are callings, there is work, and there are missions to fulfill.

I also learned that the Temple is not only a building of stone and light: it is a bridge between worlds, a place where the veil can thin and where family love keeps beating beyond death.

## A testimony of hope

Those dreams were not coincidence. They came on significant dates: it was the eve of Easter, the Resurrection of Jesus Christ, the Savior of the world, who rose from the dead and left an eternal and clear truth that all will rise again. They came with spiritual clarity and with a message of comfort that arrived personally. My father had not disappeared; he had continued to serve. And in his farewell he left me a certainty I still treasure today: on the other side the Gospel is also preached, people also minister, and the will of God is also fulfilled.

That visit—received in dreams, but engraved on the soul—became one of the most sacred treasures of my faith.
