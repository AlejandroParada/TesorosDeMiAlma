13 Lost Couple at Tres Cruces

"Where shall I go that I may obtain food?" (1 Nephi 16:23)

One day I was doing some shopping in downtown Montevideo, and there is a bus terminal, the most important one for connecting the entire country, called "Tres Cruces." There's a shopping mall at that location. I was approaching that shopping center from the other side and felt a strong impulse: "Go to the mall." I didn't pay much attention to it, and again I felt: "Go to the mall." I said, "Okay, I'll go to the mall," and when I arrived there were stairs going up to the mall and down to the terminal. I felt: "Go down," so I went in that direction. "Keep walking," I felt again. So I kept walking through a terminal full of people. "Stop," I felt, and I looked around... I saw a couple; they were speaking English. I approached them and realized they were missionaries, a missionary couple (I saw their missionary name tags). They were lost; they had just arrived, had received their first assignment, and didn't know where or what to do! So they prayed with great faith: "Please help us, oh Lord." When I reached them, I felt prompted to say: "I was sent to you" (in English). They looked at me and asked: "Do you speak English?" I said: "Yes, I do!" They looked at each other, and she said: "The Lord is watching over us!" with tears in her eyes. I said: "I'm a member of the Church," and that Elder said: "Really?" "I'm going to help you," I told them, so I helped them take the correct bus to get to their first assignment in the city of Maldonado. I am very grateful that the impulse I felt when I was a missionary is still present in my life through studying the scriptures, praying, and meditating. The whispers of the Holy Spirit, the source of joy in my life.
