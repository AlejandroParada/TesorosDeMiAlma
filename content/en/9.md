# 9 The Faith of Atilio

*“And the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up; and if he have committed sins, they shall be forgiven him.”* — James 5:15

When I learned that Atilio was hospitalized at the Hospital de Clínicas, I went immediately to visit him. That was before the 2019 pandemic; I worked nearby and could walk to the hospital without difficulty.

When I arrived, they were running several medical tests, and some family member was always present with him. The family was understandably worried, anxiously awaiting the results.

## The Diagnosis

One of those days when I arrived in the afternoon, the results of all the studies had finally come. Unfortunately, the news was not positive: it was cancer. Shortly afterward the oncologist arrived to give a detailed interpretation of all the clinical analyses.

Atilio, with a serenity that impressed me deeply, said to the doctor: “Doctor, don’t hide anything from me. Tell me the whole truth, whatever it is. I leave everything in God’s hands. Just tell me.”

The doctor, respecting that honesty and courage, explained: “Look, you have a tumor in the upper part of the kidney. We don’t know exactly what size it is. If we operate, we don’t know what we may find during surgery. If it is small, we will only remove the affected part, but if not, I will have to remove the entire kidney.”

I had never witnessed as much faith as Atilio showed when he answered: “Doctor, I trust you fully and I know you will do what is best. Do whatever you have to do!”

What complete surrender and what absolute trust.

## The Fasting Commitment

The operation was scheduled for two days later. After the doctor left the room, we stayed with Atilio at that moment, together with his daughter. That was when I felt a strong impression in my heart.

I said: “I want you to know, Atilio, that I am going to fast for you so that everything turns out well, starting today.”

His daughter, moved by the proposal, immediately said she would join in and that she too would fast for her father.

## The Medical Miracle

The operation was a complete success. Although they did have to remove the entire kidney, the surgery went very well and without any complication. It was considered “the major operation that came out impeccable and perfect,” to the astonishment of all the doctors on the surgical team.

I wondered whether the doctor knew that we had fasted for the operation to go well. I even asked Atilio if the doctor knew anything about our spiritual commitment.

Atilio answered me with a smile full of gratitude: “You have no idea what happened the day before the operation. The doctor, after his regular shift ended, came especially to see me and said: ‘I can see that you are a man of faith. I am going to fast for you!’”

I had never heard anything like that in my entire life: a doctor fasting for a patient. It was evident that the Spirit had touched his heart as well.

## Years of Blessing

Atilio lived several more years after that operation, and during that time he blessed the life of his family and of many other people in very special ways. His testimony of faith and his attitude toward trials inspired everyone who knew him.

## The Encounter in the Temple

One day I was in the Montevideo Temple, waiting for the beginning of a session (a special meeting in which we participate, similar to but different from Sunday meetings in the chapel). When I saw several people enter, one of them seemed familiar.

It was the doctor who had operated on Atilio.

I had the immense joy of seeing him there, in the House of the Lord. In that moment I knew with certainty that God had been mindful of every detail from the beginning, as He always is. It had not been chance that precisely that doctor, a man of faith, was the one who operated on Atilio.

The Lord had prepared every element of this experience: a patient with unwavering faith, a family that fasted, a believing doctor who also fasted, and a result that blessed many people through Atilio.

Sometimes miracles are not only in the healing, but in how God coordinates the hearts of people so that His will is fulfilled in the most perfect way.
