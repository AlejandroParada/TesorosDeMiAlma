# 2 A Little Girl's Faith

"If you can believe, all things are possible to him who believes." (Mark 9:23)

There was a little girl in the 1950s who was very active and energetic at 4 years old. Her parents, raised Catholic, mentioned God out of routine or tradition. Her parents had a small store and couldn't find ways to keep her entertained, so they got her a little puppy as a pet. It was very playful with its owner and full of energy. In the mornings, her puppy would go and wake up its little owner with barks and whimpering. The girl noticed many things and prayed to the "little virgin" because she had seen this from her devout parents who talked to her about God, and she knew that the most important thing was God and talking to God, even though she didn't understand the concepts like the grown-ups did.

There was one occasion in the afternoon when, due to carelessness, the puppy escaped from her side and ran to the street, and just then a truck was passing... it ran over it. Everyone was stunned—her parents went to pick up what was left of the puppy (literally crushed) and put it in a box. Her parents told her, to comfort the girl, that maybe it would be okay the next day, although they didn't want to add more sadness to the situation given the obvious reality. They set it aside and everyone went to bed because it was nighttime. The girl began to pray and said: "Dear God, Dear God, I know that you live and that you are there, please heal that puppy, please heal it!"

The next morning, before her parents' eyes, because they couldn't believe it, because they couldn't trust their ears and eyes, the puppy appeared as if nothing had happened, going to wake up its little owner with barks and whimpering. You can't imagine the joy it brought to its owner, which marked and defined her faith for her entire life. More than 70 years later, my mother shared her testimony with her children and grandchildren about how a little girl's faith can give hope and produce miracles.
