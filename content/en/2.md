# 2 A Little Girl's Faith

*"If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth."* - [Mark 9:23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/9?lang=eng&id=p23#p23)

In the nineteen-fifties there was a very active, energetic little girl of four. Her parents, raised Catholic, mentioned God out of routine or family tradition, but without a deeply rooted faith.

Her parents had a small neighborhood grocery, and, unable to find ways to keep a daughter so full of energy entertained, they decided to get her a puppy as a pet. It was a very playful little animal with its young owner, every bit as energetic as she was. In the mornings, the puppy faithfully went to wake its little mistress with happy barks and affectionate whimpers.

The girl was very observant and perceptive for her age. She prayed to the "little Virgin" because she had seen her devout parents speak of God, and she knew instinctively that what mattered most was God, and speaking with Him, even if she did not understand theological ideas the way adults did.

One afternoon, through an unavoidable moment of carelessness, the puppy slipped away from her side and ran toward the street. Just then a heavy truck was passing and could not brake in time. The vehicle ran over the little animal.

Everyone was frozen by the tragedy. Her parents, hearts broken, went to gather what remained of the puppy, which had literally been crushed. With great sorrow they carefully placed it in a cardboard box. To comfort their daughter, they told her that maybe it would be all right the next day, though they did not want to add more sadness to that terrible situation with the harsh reality that was plain to them.

They left the box at one side of the patio, and the whole family went to bed, for it was already night and the day had been too painful.

But the girl would not accept it. She began to pray with the pure, unshakable faith that only little children have, and she said: "Dear God, dear God, I know that You live and that You are there. Please, heal my puppy. Please, heal him!"

The next morning something happened that defied all logic and reason. Before the unbelieving eyes of her parents, who could not trust their own ears or eyes, the very same puppy appeared, jaunty as could be, completely well and full of life, going to wake its little owner with the same happy barks and affectionate whimpers as always.

You cannot imagine the immense joy this gave the girl, a moment that marked and defined her faith for the rest of her life. More than seventy years later, my mother still tells this testimony to her children and grandchildren, reminding us how the sincere faith of a little girl can give hope and bring about true miracles.

This experience taught her that when we draw near to God with a pure heart and genuine faith, without the doubts and limitations we often develop as adults, He can work wonders that go beyond our human understanding.

Sometimes the most powerful faith is the simplest.
