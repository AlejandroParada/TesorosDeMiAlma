26 Call Home!!!

"...I will be on your right hand and on your left, and my Spirit shall be in your hearts, and mine angels round about you, to bear you up" (D&C 84:88)

My father had always taught us that if we had a problem, we should call home.

On one occasion before my mission, I worked at an ice cream factory all night and we were in charge of washing the molds that were used during the day. My coworkers knew I had little time left and wanted to throw me a farewell party like those of the "world," and some people would come, specially invited women for that occasion.

I worried because it wouldn't be a good or appropriate party because of all the atmosphere that was being generated and the activities they planned to do. There was no supervisor at night so almost anything could happen. So I thought, "What do I do?" and I felt the impression to go to the back of the factory—besides, we were few people. In the back there was an office they weren't using that they were using as storage, and I saw it was full of empty cardboard boxes and there was also a desk covered by boxes that could barely be seen. When I got closer I saw a telephone among the boxes and felt the strong impression "Call home! Call home!" I picked up the receiver and it had a dial tone. I dialed my home number and my mother answered (it was already about 3 am) and she said, "Are you okay?" Yes, I responded. I asked if she could wake up Dad and she put him on. I explained the matter and my concern, and he responded that I should stay calm, that he would resolve it. His words calmed my worries. Not much time passed and one of the coworkers shouted that they wouldn't be able to have the party they planned because a supervisor had announced he would come by the factory that night, which was very uncommon for him to visit. In the end everything turned out well and there was no party because the supervisor came and they behaved normally and well.

The next day when I could see my father in the afternoon after he returned from work, he asked me how that work day had been, and I thanked him: "Thank you, Dad, for calling the supervisor," to which he responded, "I didn't call the supervisor, nor do I know his phone number." So I asked him, "What did you do after I called you?" He responded, "What I did was go wake up your brothers and we got on our knees and asked that you be protected, nothing more." This filled me with gratitude to God for His mercy in having protected me that night.

On another occasion days later, they asked at work where there would be a telephone to make a call, to which I responded, "In the back there's an office that has a telephone," and some looked at me strangely and said, "In the back office there's a broken telephone and besides there's no phone connection." This filled my heart even more with gratitude to God for that miracle and "his tender mercies" (1 Nephi 1:8) toward me in protecting me that day. This reaffirms my testimony even more of the certainty that "whosoever shall put their trust in God shall be supported in their trials, and their troubles, and their afflictions..." (Alma 36:3)
