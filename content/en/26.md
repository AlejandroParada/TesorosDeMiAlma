# 26 Call Home!

> **"And moreover, verily I say unto you, that if ye rejoice and are grateful before God in your tribulations, the Lord will give you rest from all your enemies."** ([Doctrine and Covenants 78:18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/78?lang=eng&id=p18#p18))

> **"I will be on your right hand and on your left, and my Spirit shall be in your hearts, and mine angels round about you, to bear you up."** ([Doctrine and Covenants 84:88](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/84?lang=eng&id=p88#p88))

## A wise father's teaching

My father had always taught us a fundamental principle: "If you have a problem, call home." This was not only practical advice, but a deep teaching about the importance of seeking wise counsel and family prayer when we face difficulties. I never imagined how literal and miraculous this teaching would turn out to be.

## A compromising situation before the mission

On one occasion, shortly before I left on my mission, I worked at an ice cream factory all night long. My job was to wash the molds that were used during the day, work that allowed me to save money for my missionary service.

My coworkers knew I had little time left before leaving to serve the Lord, and they wanted to throw me a farewell party. Their idea of a "party," however, was completely different from mine. They planned to bring invited people, especially women, for a celebration that clearly would not be appropriate for a future missionary.

## The worry and the search for guidance

I was deeply worried, because it would not be a good or appropriate party, given the whole atmosphere that was building and the activities they had planned. I knew there was no supervisor during the night, which meant that practically anything could happen without immediate consequences.

Then I asked myself: "What do I do?" In that moment of uncertainty, I felt a clear impression from the Holy Ghost: I should go to the back of the factory. Besides, there were few of us working that night, which made it more possible to follow that inspiration.

## The miraculous discovery

At the back of the factory there was an office they did not use and were using as a storeroom. When I went in, I saw that it was full of empty cardboard boxes stacked everywhere. There was also a desk almost completely covered by the boxes, barely visible.

As I drew near and looked among the boxes, I discovered something unexpected: a telephone. In that moment I felt an even stronger and clearer spiritual impression: **"Call home! Call home!"**

## The call for help

I picked up the receiver and, to my surprise, there was a dial tone. I dialed my home number, and my mother answered. It was already about 3:00 in the morning, but she answered at once with a mother's concern: "Are you all right?"

"Yes," I answered, and then I asked if she could wake Dad. She put him on, and I explained the whole situation and my worry about the inappropriate party they wanted to throw for me.

My father listened carefully and answered with the wisdom and calm that always marked him: "Stay calm. I am going to take care of this." His words immediately settled all my worries. I felt a deep peace, knowing that my father would intercede in some way.

## Divine intervention

Not long after that call, one of my coworkers shouted in frustration that they were no longer going to be able to have the party they had planned. A supervisor had given notice that he would stop by the factory that night, something that was extremely uncommon.

In the end, everything turned out well. There was no inappropriate party, because the supervisor showed up, and everyone behaved in a normal and appropriate way. The situation that had worried me so much was resolved in a completely unexpected way.

## The revelation about the true miracle

The next day, when I was able to see my father in the afternoon after he came home from work, he asked me how that workday had gone. I thanked him warmly: "Thank you, Dad, for having called the supervisor."

His answer left me completely astonished: "I did not call the supervisor. I do not even know his phone number."

Confused, I asked him: "Then what did you do after I called you?"

His answer revealed the true source of the miracle: "What I did was go wake your brothers, and we knelt down and asked the Lord to protect you. Nothing more."

## A heart full of gratitude

These words filled my heart with immense gratitude to God for His mercy in protecting me that night. It had not been my father's human intervention that resolved the situation, but the power of family prayer and the Lord's direct intervention.

## Confirmation of the miracle

Days later, the miraculous nature of what had happened became even clearer. When some coworkers asked at work where they could find a telephone to make a call, I answered: "In the back there is an office that has a telephone."

Some looked at me strangely and said: "In the back office there is a broken telephone, and besides, there is no phone line there."

## The tender mercies of the Lord

This revelation swelled my heart even more with gratitude to God for that miracle and for His **"tender mercies"** ([1 Nephi 1:20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/1?lang=eng&id=p20#p20)) toward me in protecting me that day. The telephone I had used to call home was not only supposedly broken; it did not even have a phone connection.

## A strengthened testimony

This experience reaffirmed even more my testimony of the certainty of the scriptures that declare: **"And whosoever shall put their trust in God shall be supported in their trials, and their troubles, and their afflictions, and shall be lifted up at the last day"** ([Alma 36:3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/36?lang=eng&id=p3#p3)).

## Eternal lessons

This experience taught me several deep lessons:

### **The power of family prayer**
The united prayer of my father and my brothers called down the blessings of heaven in an immediate and powerful way. They did not need to make phone calls or use earthly means; they simply knelt before the Lord in faith.

### **The importance of following spiritual impressions**
When I felt the impression to go to the back of the factory and then to call home, I could have doubted or rationalized. But by following those impressions of the Holy Ghost, I placed myself in a position to receive divine help.

### **Miracles still happen**
In our day, the Lord continues to manifest His power to protect and bless His children. Miracles are not only ancient accounts, but present realities for those who live with faith.

### **Divine protection is real**
The Lord has ways of protecting us that go beyond our understanding. He can use supervisors, miraculous telephones, and seemingly chance circumstances to fulfill His purposes.

## Final reflection

When I remember this experience, my heart fills with wonder at the Lord's **"tender mercies."** He knew my situation, my worry, and my desire to remain worthy to serve a mission. He provided a telephone that worked when it should not have worked, and He orchestrated circumstances for my protection through the prayers of my family.

My father's teaching—"call home"—took on a much deeper meaning. It was not only about seeking human counsel, but about connecting with a chain of faith that reaches to heaven. When we call home in faith, our Heavenly Father always answers, though His answers may come in ways we could never imagine.
