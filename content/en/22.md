# I Had Never Felt So Much Silence

> **“And it came to pass that he went and got upon the wall thereof, and stretched forth his hand and cried with a loud voice, and prophesied unto the people whatsoever things the Lord put into his heart.”** ([Helaman 13:4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/hel/13?lang=eng&id=p4#p4))

## The Unexpected Moment

There was a moment at a job I had some years ago. I was in a large, bustling office, full of desktop computers and numerous employees carrying out their daily work: some programming, others working with Excel spreadsheets, and several more drafting documents in Word. We all worked in individual cubicles; there were about fifty of us in an office located on the ground floor of a corporate building.

## A Question That Changed Everything

At a certain moment, for reasons I still do not know, my coworkers began to talk about religion. They knew I was a member of the Church because I had previously explained to them the reasons why I do not drink alcohol or smoke cigarettes.

Suddenly, one of them asked me out loud: “What do you think about Mormons believing that Joseph Smith was a prophet?”

## The Deafening Silence

In that precise instant, I had never experienced the silence of that office as I perceived it that day. Not a sound was heard; the entire office sank into an almost absolute silence, as if everyone were waiting expectantly for my answer to that so direct question.

## My Testimony in the Workplace

I shared my sincere testimony of the Prophet Joseph Smith with conviction and clarity. What I experienced that day will remain engraved in my memory forever. I fervently hope that my coworkers not only heard my words that day in that place, but that they also felt the Spirit that accompanied them.

## Lessons Learned About Testimony

I have come to understand a fundamental truth: my mission to testify of the Gospel, of Jesus Christ, and of the prophets is not limited only to dramatic moments like that one.

Most of the time I fulfill this sacred responsibility through quiet actions of love and service every day. Yet I also do it openly in The Church of Jesus Christ, of which I know with certainty that He lives and that prophets continue to guide His Church in our day.

## Final Reflection

That moment of silence taught me that the Spirit can transform any everyday situation into a sacred opportunity to testify. No matter how unexpected the moment may be; when we are spiritually prepared, the Lord provides the necessary words and the courage to share our faith with authenticity and power.
