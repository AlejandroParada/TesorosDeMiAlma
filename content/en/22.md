22 I Had Never Felt Such Silence

"...he went and got upon the wall...he cried with a loud voice...the things which the Lord put into his heart" (Helaman 13:4)

There was a moment at a job I had several years ago. I was in a large, noisy office with many desktop computers and many people doing their work—many programming, others with Excel spreadsheets, and others writing in Word. Everyone worked in cubicles, about 50 people, in a ground-floor office of a building. At one point, I don't know how, my coworkers started talking about religion (they knew I was a member of the Church because I had explained why I don't drink alcohol or smoke). And one of them asked me loudly: "What do you think about Mormons believing that Joseph Smith was a prophet?" And at that moment I had never felt the silence in that office like I felt it that day—there was no noise, and the entire office fell into almost complete silence, as if waiting for my response to that question. I gave my sincere testimony about the prophet Joseph Smith, and what I felt that day I will never forget, and I hope my coworkers heard it that day in that place, and surely they felt it! I have learned that my mission was not to testify of the gospel, of Jesus Christ, and of the prophets only at the last moment; most of the time I do it through quiet acts of love each day, although I have also done it in the Church of Jesus Christ, of which I know that He lives, and that prophets guide His Church today.
