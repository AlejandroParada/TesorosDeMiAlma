# Mission President

## Definition
A **mission president** is a member of the Church called by the General Authorities to preside over a specific mission and to supervise the missionary work in that geographic area for approximately three years.

## Calling and service
- **Duration**: Generally three years
- **Calling**: By a member of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles
- **Service**: Full-time, with his wife
- **Sustaining**: Sustained by the members of the Church

## Principal responsibilities

### **Supervision of missionaries**
- Direct between 100 and 400 full-time missionaries
- Conduct regular progress interviews
- Provide training and spiritual guidance
- Assign companions and areas of service

### **Mission administration**
- Supervise proselyting work throughout the mission
- Coordinate with local stake and ward leaders
- Administer missionary resources and budgets
- Report regularly to Area authorities

### **Spiritual leadership**
- Teach and train in principles of the gospel
- Give priesthood blessings
- Provide counsel and guidance under inspiration
- Be an example of faith and dedication

### **Pastoral care**
- Watch over the physical and emotional welfare of the missionaries
- Attend to medical and health needs
- Give support in difficult times
- Help with cultural and language adjustment

## The mission president's wife
- **Full partner**: She serves alongside her husband
- **Mission mother**: She cares especially for the sister missionaries
- **Trainer**: She teaches gospel principles and missionary skills
- **Counselor**: She provides maternal guidance and support

## Mission home
- **Temporary home**: For missionaries in transition
- **Training center**: A place of orientation and instruction
- **Refuge**: For missionaries who are ill or have special needs
- **Example**: A model of a Christian home centered on Christ

## Essential qualities
- **Leadership experience**: A background of service in the Church
- **Spiritual maturity**: A firm testimony of the gospel
- **Administrative skills**: The ability to direct organizations
- **Pastoral sensitivity**: Genuine love for young adults

## Impact and legacy
- **Spiritual development**: He influences hundreds of young missionaries
- **Growth of the Church**: He supervises the expansion of the gospel
- **Preparation of leaders**: He helps form future leaders of the Church
- **Strengthening of families**: He models Christian family principles

The mission president serves as shepherd, teacher, administrator, and spiritual father to the missionaries in his care, and is a vital instrument in the Lord's work of salvation.
