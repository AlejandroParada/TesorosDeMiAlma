# Bishop

**Local congregation leader**

A bishop is the ecclesiastical leader of a local congregation (ward) of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. He is called by inspiration and serves as pastor and common judge in Israel for his congregation.

## Main responsibilities:

- **Pastor**: Spiritually care for ward members
- **Aaronic Priesthood president**: Lead the young men
- **Welfare**: Administer aid to families in need
- **Common judge**: Conduct worthiness and disciplinary interviews
- **Administration**: Supervise ward organizations

## Calling characteristics:

- **Volunteer service**: Receives no financial compensation
- **Duration**: Generally serves 3 to 5 years
- **Part-time**: Maintains secular employment
- **Two counselors**: Form a ward bishopric
- **Executive secretary**: Administrative support

## Typical activities:

- Preside over Sunday meetings
- Personal interviews with members
- Supervision of ward programs
- Coordination with auxiliary organizations
- Distribution of charitable aid

## Interviews:

Members meet regularly with the bishop to:
- Renew temple recommends
- Seek spiritual counsel
- Confess transgressions if necessary
- Receive service callings

The bishop serves with love and dedication, being an example of Christian service in the community.
