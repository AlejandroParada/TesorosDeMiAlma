# Mission

**Full-time missionary service**

A mission is a period of full-time religious service, generally 18 to 24 months, where young Latter-day Saints dedicate their time to preaching the gospel of Jesus Christ and serving others.

## Mission characteristics:

- **Duration**: 24 months for men, 18 months for women
- **Age**: Men from age 18, women from age 19
- **Call**: Specific assignment to a region of the world
- **Companions**: Always work in pairs of the same gender
- **Full dedication**: Full time dedicated to service

## Main activities:

- Teach the gospel to those who are interested
- Serve in the community and help those in need
- Work with local Church members
- Study scriptures and prepare lessons
- Participate in welfare and humanitarian service work

## Preparation:

Future missionaries prepare spiritually, physically, mentally, and socially. They learn languages if necessary and receive training in missionary training centers.

The missionary experience strengthens personal faith and develops leadership and service skills that last a lifetime.
