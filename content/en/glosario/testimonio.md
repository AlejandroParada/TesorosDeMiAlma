# Testimony

## Definition
In the context of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, a **testimony** is a personal spiritual knowledge and confirmation from the Holy Spirit about the truthfulness of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

## Characteristics of testimony
- **Personal knowledge**: It is an individual revelation that comes from the Holy Spirit
- **Spiritual confirmation**: Goes beyond intellectual knowledge
- **Deep conviction**: Provides certainty about Gospel truths
- **Sacred experience**: It is a spiritual gift that strengthens faith

## How is it obtained?
1. **Sincere study**: Scripture study and studying the teachings of prophets
2. **Fervent prayer**: Ask God with faith and sincere heart
3. **Obedience**: Live the commandments and principles of the Gospel
4. **Spiritual experiences**: Be open to the manifestations of the Spirit

## How is it shared?
- **With simplicity**: Express what one feels and knows
- **With conviction**: Declare truths with spiritual certainty
- **With love**: Share from the heart with genuine desire to bless
- **By the Spirit**: Allow the Holy Spirit to confirm the words

## Related scriptures
> *"For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation"* (Romans 1:16)

## Importance in a member's life
Testimony is the foundation of faith and the source of spiritual strength. It is what motivates members to serve, obey, and share the Gospel with others.
