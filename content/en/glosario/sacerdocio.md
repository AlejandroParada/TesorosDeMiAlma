# Priesthood

## Definition
The **priesthood** is the eternal authority and power of God, conferred upon worthy men on earth to act in His name and to administer the ordinances of salvation and exaltation.

## The two orders of the priesthood

### **Aaronic Priesthood** (Lesser Priesthood)
- **Offices**: Deacon, Teacher, Priest, Bishop
- **Keys**: The ministering of angels, the preparatory gospel, baptism by immersion
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Administer the sacrament
  - Collect fast offerings
  - Care for temporal needs
  - Teach, exhort, and baptize

### **Melchizedek Priesthood** (Higher Priesthood)
- **Offices**: Elder, High Priest, Patriarch, Seventy, Apostle
- **Keys**: All the spiritual blessings of the Church
- **Responsibilities**:
  - Administer temple ordinances
  - Blessings of health and comfort
  - Confirm members and confer the gift of the Holy Ghost
  - Direct missionary work

## Fundamental principles

### **Divine authority**
- Conferred by the laying on of hands by someone who already holds it
- An unbroken line of authority from the original Apostles
- Restored through the prophet Joseph Smith

### **Personal worthiness**
- Requires living in accordance with the commandments
- It is lost when there is serious transgression
- It is retained through continued righteousness

### **Selfless service**
- It is given to bless others, not for personal benefit
- "No power or influence can or ought to be maintained by virtue of the priesthood" ([Doctrine and Covenants 121:41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/121?lang=eng&id=p41#p41))

## Ordinances of the priesthood
1. **Baptism** by immersion (Aaronic Priesthood)
2. **Confirmation** and the gift of the Holy Ghost (Melchizedek Priesthood)
3. **Ordination** to the priesthood
4. **Blessing** and anointing of the sick
5. **Temple ordinances** (endowment, sealing)

## Related scriptures
> *"And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven"* ([Matthew 16:19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/16?lang=eng&id=p19#p19))

> *"And thus they become high priests forever, after the order of the Son, the Only Begotten of the Father, who is without beginning of days or end of years"* ([Alma 13:9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/13?lang=eng&id=p9#p9))

## Blessings of the priesthood
- **For the one who holds it**: Spiritual growth, opportunities to serve
- **For families**: Patriarchal leadership, a father's blessings
- **For the Church**: Orderly administration, divine authority
- **For the world**: Preaching of the gospel, ordinances of salvation

## Organization of the priesthood
- **Quorums**: Groups organized by priesthood office
- **Keys**: Specific authority to direct the work of the Lord
- **Presidency**: Orderly leadership at every level

The priesthood is the means by which God blesses His children and administers His kingdom on the earth.
