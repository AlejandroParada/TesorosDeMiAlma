# Temple

**House of the Lord - Sacred building**

Temples are the most sacred buildings of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. They are literally considered "houses of the Lord" where sacred ordinances and eternal covenants are performed.

## Purpose of temples:

- **Sacred ordinances**: Ceremonies that unite families for eternity
- **Worship**: Place of prayer, meditation, and personal revelation
- **Service for the dead**: Vicarious ordinances for deceased ancestors
- **Covenants**: Sacred promises between the person and God
- **Spiritual peace**: Refuge from the world to find divine direction

## Main ordinances:

- **Endowment**: Ceremony of sacred covenants and promises
- **Sealing**: Eternal marriage and sealing of families
- **Baptisms for the dead**: Vicarious ordinance for ancestors
- **Confirmations**: Gift of the Holy Ghost for the deceased

## Requirements to enter:

- Be a worthy member of the Church
- Hold a valid temple recommend
- Live gospel standards
- Interviews with ecclesiastical leaders

## Special characteristics:

- **Holiness**: Atmosphere of reverence and spirituality
- **Beauty**: Architecture and decoration that inspire
- **White clothing**: Symbolizes purity and equality
- **Silence**: Place of peace away from worldly noise

Temples represent the connection between heaven and earth, where families can be united forever.
