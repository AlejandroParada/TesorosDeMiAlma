# Relief Society

**Women's organization of The Church of Jesus Christ**

Relief Society is one of the largest and oldest women's organizations in the world. It was organized in 1842 by the prophet Joseph Smith for the adult women of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

## Purpose:

"To relieve the poor and needy, and save souls" - these words from the prophet Joseph Smith define the mission of Relief Society since its beginnings.

## Main activities:

- **Charitable service**: Help for families and people in need
- **Spiritual teaching**: Sunday classes and gospel studies
- **Sisterhood**: Strengthening bonds among sisters
- **Welfare**: Care for mothers, widows, and single women
- **Temple work**: Participation in sacred ordinances

## Motto:

"Charity never faileth" ([1 Corinthians 13:8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/13?lang=eng&id=p8#p8))

## Organization:

- **President**: Leads the local organization
- **Counselors**: Support in responsibilities
- **Secretary**: Keeps records and communications
- **Visiting teachers**: Care for assigned families

Relief Society strengthens women in their faith, family, and service, creating a network of support and mutual care in each community.
