# Prayer

**Prayer** is reverent communication with our Heavenly Father. It is an essential and constant practice in the lives of members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

## What is prayer?

- **Direct communication** with God as our Heavenly Father
- **Sincere and reverent** spiritual conversation
- **Expression of faith** in Jesus Christ and His atonement
- **Means of receiving** divine guidance, comfort, and strength

## Elements of prayer:

1. **Address God**: "Heavenly Father" or "Our Father who art in heaven"
2. **Gratitude**: Thank Him for blessings received
3. **Petitions**: Ask for help, guidance, blessings for oneself and others
4. **Confession**: Repent of mistakes and ask for forgiveness
5. **Closing**: "In the name of Jesus Christ, Amen"

## Types of prayer:

- **Personal**: Individual, in private
- **Family**: With family gathered together
- **Public**: In Church meetings
- **Blessings**: Special prayers with laying on of hands
- **Dedicatory**: To consecrate sacred places or things

## When to pray:

- Morning and evening
- Before meals
- In times of need or crisis
- When making important decisions
- In gratitude for blessings received
- Whenever the heart feels moved to pray

## Promised blessings:

God has promised to hear and answer our prayers according to His wisdom and in His due time. Prayer brings peace, comfort, guidance, and strengthens our personal relationship with Him.
