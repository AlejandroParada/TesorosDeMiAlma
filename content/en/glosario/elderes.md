# Elders

**Full-time male missionaries**

Elders are young men called to serve a full-time mission in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The title "Elder" comes from the Melchizedek Priesthood office they hold during their missionary service.

## Main characteristics:

- **Age**: Generally begin at age 18 or older
- **Duration**: Usually serve 24 months
- **Companions**: Always work in pairs (companions)
- **Identification**: Wear a name tag with their surname and the name of the Church
- **Call**: Receive an assignment to a specific mission in the world

## Typical activities:

- Teach the gospel of Jesus Christ
- Invite people to be baptized
- Serve in the community
- Strengthen local members
- Study the scriptures and prepare each day

## Difference from the priesthood office:

In the Church, "elder" is also a priesthood office. In the missionary context, "the elders" specifically refers to full-time male missionaries.

Elders represent Jesus Christ and dedicate their time to preaching His gospel and serving others.
