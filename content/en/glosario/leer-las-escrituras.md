# Scripture study

**Scripture study** is a fundamental and constant practice of members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

## The scriptures include:

- **The Bible**: Old and New Testament
- **The Book of Mormon**: Another Testament of Jesus Christ
- **Doctrine and Covenants**: Modern revelations
- **The Pearl of Great Price**: Additional writings of great value

## Why study the scriptures?

- To know God's will
- To receive personal inspiration and guidance
- To strengthen faith in Jesus Christ
- To find answers to life's problems
- To feel the Holy Spirit
- To prepare spiritually each day

## Common practices:

- **Daily personal study**: Preferably in the mornings
- **Family study**: In regular family meetings
- **Group study**: In Sunday classes and seminaries
- **Memorization**: Of significant verses
- **Meditation**: Reflecting on principles learned

## Promised blessings:

Those who read the scriptures regularly are promised greater inspiration, spiritual protection, wisdom to make decisions, and a stronger testimony of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Scripture study is considered essential "spiritual food" for the growth of the soul.
