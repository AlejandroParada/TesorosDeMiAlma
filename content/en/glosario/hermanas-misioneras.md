# Sister missionaries

**Full-time female missionaries**

Sister missionaries are young women called to serve a full-time mission in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. They dedicate their time to preaching the gospel of Jesus Christ and serving people.

## Main characteristics:

- **Age**: Generally begin at age 19 or older
- **Duration**: Usually serve 18 months
- **Companions**: Always work in pairs (companions)
- **Identification**: Wear a name tag with their surname and the name of the Church
- **Call**: Receive an assignment to a specific mission in the world

## Typical activities:

- Teach the gospel of Jesus Christ
- Invite people to be baptized
- Serve in the community
- Strengthen members and families
- Study the scriptures and prepare each day

## Relationship with the elders:

Elders and sister missionaries serve together in the same mission, but they work in same-gender pairs. Both share the same purpose: to bring the Savior's message to all people.

Sister missionaries bring faith, testimony, and a great spirit of service to every area where they serve.
