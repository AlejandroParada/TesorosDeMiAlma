# Christ (Jesus Christ)

## Definition
**Christ** is the title that means "The Anointed One" or "The Messiah." **Jesus Christ** is the literal Son of God in the flesh, the Savior and Redeemer of the world, who came to earth to atone for the sins of mankind and to offer eternal life to all who believe in Him.

## Divine nature and mission

### **Son of God**
- **Literal Son of Heavenly Father**: Begotten of God in the flesh
- **Only Begotten of the Father**: The only Son begotten of the Father in the flesh
- **Dual nature**: Fully divine and fully human
- **Without sin**: He lived a perfect life, without transgression

### **The promised Savior**
- **Foretold from Adam**: Announced by prophets from the beginning
- **Fulfillment of prophecy**: All messianic prophecies were fulfilled in Him
- **The Lamb of God**: The perfect sacrifice for the Atonement
- **Conqueror of death**: He was resurrected on the third day

## Sacred titles and names

### **Doctrinal titles**
- **The Christ**: The Anointed One, the promised Messiah
- **The Savior**: He who saves from death and sin
- **The Redeemer**: He who rescues us from the Fall
- **The Mediator**: He intercedes between God and man
- **The Advocate**: He pleads before the Father for the penitent

### **Descriptive names**
- **Prince of Peace**: He brings peace to the human heart
- **King of Kings**: Supreme Sovereign
- **Light of the World**: He enlightens every person
- **Good Shepherd**: He cares for and guides His sheep
- **The Way, the Truth, and the Life**: The only way to the Father

## The Atonement of Jesus Christ

### **The infinite sacrifice**
- **Gethsemane**: He suffered for the sins of all mankind
- **Calvary**: He gave His life on the cross
- **The tomb**: He overcame death through the Resurrection
- **Infinite price**: He satisfied the demands of justice

### **Blessings of the Atonement**
- **Forgiveness of sins**: Through repentance
- **Universal Resurrection**: All will be resurrected
- **Eternal life**: For the obedient and faithful
- **Peace and healing**: Comfort in affliction

## Perfect example

### **Mortal life**
- **Perfect obedience**: He always did the will of the Father
- **Unconditional love**: He loved everyone without exception
- **Selfless service**: He ministered to all who were in need
- **Humility**: Despite His divinity, He served with meekness

### **Teachings**
- **The gospel**: He taught the plan of salvation
- **Eternal principles**: Love, forgiveness, service, faith
- **Parables**: He taught profound truths in a simple way
- **Personal example**: His actions supported His words

## The Light of Christ

### **Universal light**
- **Conscience**: The capacity to discern between good and evil
- **Divine influence**: It invites every person to do good
- **Spiritual guide**: It leads toward truth and righteousness
- **Universal gift**: Given to every person who comes into the world

### **How to follow His light**
- **Live His teachings**: Obey His commandments
- **Imitate His example**: Love and serve as He did
- **Seek His Spirit**: Through prayer and study
- **Share His love**: Minister to others with charity

## Personal relationship with Christ

### **Faith in Christ**
- **Know His divine nature**: Study about Him in the scriptures
- **Trust in His power**: Believe that He can save us
- **Follow His example**: Live as He lived
- **Call upon His name**: Pray to the Father in His name

### **Becoming like Christ**
- **Develop Christlike attributes**: Love, patience, humility, forgiveness
- **Serve others**: As He served selflessly
- **Forgive**: As He forgives our sins
- **Love unconditionally**: As He loves us

## Testimony of Christ
A testimony of Jesus Christ is the sure knowledge, given by the Holy Ghost, that Jesus is the Christ, the Savior of the world. This testimony is the foundation of all true faith and the source of eternal peace and hope.

## His role in the restored Church
In The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, Christ is:
- **The head of the Church**: He directs His Church through living prophets
- **The center of the gospel**: All doctrine centers on Him
- **The object of worship**: We worship the Father in His name
- **Our example**: The perfect pattern to follow

Jesus Christ is the central figure of the plan of salvation and the source of all hope, peace, and eternal life. To know Him, love Him, and follow Him is the supreme purpose of mortal existence.
