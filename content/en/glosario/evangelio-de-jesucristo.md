# The Gospel of Jesus Christ

**The Gospel of Jesus Christ** is the divine plan of happiness and salvation, revealed by Jesus Christ and restored in its fullness through The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

## LDS concept:

- **The true Church**: The only organization on earth with complete divine authority
- **Restoration**: The Gospel was restored through the prophet Joseph Smith
- **Fullness**: Contains all truths, ordinances, and authority necessary for salvation
- **Personal testimony**: Confirmation by the Holy Spirit that it is true

## Fundamental elements:

1. **Faith in Jesus Christ** as Savior and Redeemer
2. **Repentance** from sins
3. **Baptism by immersion** by priesthood authority
4. **Receiving the Holy Ghost** through confirmation
5. **Enduring to the end** in obedience to commandments

## Unique characteristics:

- **Continuing revelation**: God continues to speak through living prophets
- **Priesthood authority**: Divine power restored to act in God's name
- **Sacred ordinances**: Baptism, confirmation, temple ordinances
- **Divine organization**: Apostles, prophets, and revealed organizational structure

## Personal testimony:

LDS members believe they can receive a personal **testimony** from the Holy Spirit that confirms:
- That Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God
- That Joseph Smith was a prophet called by God
- That The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is the true church
- That the Book of Mormon is the word of God

## Purpose:

The Gospel offers the way to return to God's presence and obtain eternal life, providing certainty, peace, and direction in this life and hope for eternity.
