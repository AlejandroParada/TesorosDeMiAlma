# Ward

**Local congregation of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**

A ward is the local congregational unit of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It is the equivalent of a local parish or congregation, and gathers members who live in a determined geographic area.

## Main characteristics:

- **Leadership**: Presided over by a bishop and two counselors
- **Size**: Generally between 150 and 500 members
- **Location**: Based on geographic boundaries of the community
- **Meetings**: Gather each Sunday to worship and learn
- **Organizations**: Includes Primary, Relief Society, Youth, and Priesthood

## Typical activities:

- Sunday sacrament meeting
- Sunday School classes
- Youth and family activities
- Community service and welfare
- Ministering visits to families

## Relationship with other units:

Several wards form a **stake**, which is the larger administrative unit at the regional level. In areas with fewer members, the equivalent unit is called a **branch**.

The ward is the center of daily Church life: there faith is strengthened, neighbors are served, and a community of mutual support is built.
