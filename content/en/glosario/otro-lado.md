# Other side

**"Other side"** is a common expression among members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints to refer to life after death, especially the spirit world.

## Doctrinal concept:

- **Spirit world**: The place where souls go after physical death
- **Continuity of life**: Existence doesn't end with death, but continues
- **Temporary state**: Between death and final resurrection
- **Place of learning**: Where spirits continue progressing

## Division of the spirit world:

### Spirit paradise:
- For the righteous who accepted the Gospel
- State of peace, rest, and happiness
- Place of teaching and missionary service

### Spirit prison:
- For those who didn't have opportunity to hear or accept the Gospel
- Place of learning and preparation
- Where the Gospel is preached to spirits

## Important teachings:

- **Vicarious work**: The living can perform ordinances for the dead
- **Evangelization**: Righteous spirits teach those who need the Gospel
- **Preparation**: All prepare for resurrection and final judgment
- **Eternal families**: Family relationships can continue

## Christian hope:

The doctrine of the "other side" provides hope and comfort, teaching that:
- Death is not the end
- There are opportunities for progress after death
- Families can be together forever
- God's justice and mercy extend to all

This terminology reflects faith in eternal life and the plan of salvation.
