# The Book of Mormon

**The Book of Mormon** is one of the canonical books of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, considered "Another Testament of Jesus Christ".

## What is The Book of Mormon?

- **Sacred record** of ancient inhabitants of the Americas
- **Additional testament** of Jesus Christ and His divinity
- **Translation** performed by Joseph Smith through the power of God
- **Companion** to the Bible in testifying of Christ

## Main content:

- **History**: Families who came from Jerusalem to America (600 B.C.)
- **Teachings**: Pure doctrine of the Gospel of Jesus Christ
- **Prophecies**: About Christ, His ministry, and Second Coming
- **Christ's visit**: His ministry among the Nephites after His resurrection

## Purpose:

1. Testify that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God
2. Teach the doctrine of Christ and His Atonement
3. Gather scattered Israel in the latter days
4. Confirm the truth of the Bible
5. Reveal the restored gospel

## Moroni's Promise:

*"And when ye shall receive these things, I would exhort you that ye would ask God, the Eternal Father, in the name of Christ, if these things are not true; and if ye shall ask with a sincere heart, with real intent, having faith in Christ, he will manifest the truth of it unto you, by the power of the Holy Ghost"* ([Moroni 10:4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/10?lang=eng&id=p4#p4)).

## Importance for members:

Members of the Church study The Book of Mormon regularly as a source of inspiration, doctrine, and personal testimony of Jesus Christ. It is considered "the keystone of our religion".
