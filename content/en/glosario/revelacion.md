# Revelation

## Definition
**Revelation** is God's communication with His children through the Holy Ghost. It is the means by which the Lord imparts truth, knowledge, guidance, and comfort to those who seek His will with faith and sincerity.

## Types of revelation

### **Continuing revelation**
- **For the Church**: Through prophets, seers, and revelators
- **For individuals**: Personal guidance for each member
- **For families**: Direction for parents and family leaders
- **For callings**: Inspiration to serve in specific positions

### **Forms of revelation**
1. **Spiritual impressions**: Feelings of peace, confirmation, or warning
2. **Inspired thoughts**: Ideas that come to mind during prayer
3. **Dreams and visions**: Special spiritual experiences
4. **Spoken words**: Through inspired priesthood leaders
5. **Directed circumstances**: Events that lead toward truth

## Principles for receiving revelation

### **Personal preparation**
- **Live worthily**: Obey the commandments
- **Study the scriptures**: Read the scriptures regularly
- **Pray in faith**: Communicate sincerely with God
- **Fast**: Seek greater spiritual sensitivity

### **Right attitude**
- **Humility**: Acknowledge dependence on God
- **Patience**: Wait upon the Lord's timing
- **Obedience**: Willingness to follow the guidance received
- **Gratitude**: Acknowledge the blessings of God

## Characteristics of true revelation

### **Divine source**
- It comes from God through the Holy Ghost
- It is in harmony with the scriptures
- It edifies and lifts spiritually
- It promotes righteousness and truth

### **Edifying purpose**
- It strengthens faith and testimony
- It guides in important decisions
- It provides comfort in affliction
- It reveals truths of the gospel

## How to recognize revelation

### **Spiritual feelings**
- Deep peace and calm
- Warmth in the heart (a burning in the bosom)
- Mental clarity and understanding
- A gentle but firm confirmation

### **Spiritual fruits**
- It increases faith and hope
- It promotes love and compassion
- It inspires righteous actions
- It brings joy and spiritual satisfaction

## Revelation and agency
- God respects our decisions
- Revelation guides but does not compel
- We must exercise faith in order to act
- Consequences follow our choices

## Eternal purpose
Personal revelation:
- Draws us nearer to our Heavenly Father
- Strengthens our relationship with Jesus Christ
- Prepares us for eternal life
- Enables us to serve and bless others

Revelation is one of the most precious gifts God grants His children, allowing them to navigate life with divine guidance and eternal purpose.
