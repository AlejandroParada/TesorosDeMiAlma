# Holy Ghost

## Definition
The **Holy Ghost** is the third member of the Godhead. He is a personage of spirit, without a physical body of flesh and bones, who acts as a witness of the Father and the Son and as the revealer of all truth.

## Divine nature
- **Member of the Godhead**: He forms part of the Godhead together with Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ
- **Personage of spirit**: He possesses a perfect spiritual form
- **Omnipresent**: His influence can be everywhere
- **Holy**: Completely pure and without sin

## Roles and functions

### **The Comforter**
- Brings peace in times of affliction
- Comforts the sorrowing and the afflicted
- Strengthens during trials
- Gives hope in adversity

### **Witness of truth**
- Confirms the truthfulness of the gospel of Jesus Christ
- Bears witness that Jesus is the Christ
- Reveals spiritual truths
- Helps discern between good and evil

### **Teacher and guide**
- Teaches all things necessary for salvation
- Brings to remembrance the teachings of Christ
- Guides in making important decisions
- Inspires in the study of the scriptures

### **Sanctifier**
- Purifies the heart from sin
- Helps in the process of repentance
- Transforms human nature
- Prepares us for the presence of God

## Gifts of the Holy Ghost

### **For all members**
- **Gift of the Holy Ghost**: Constant companionship after baptism and confirmation
- **Personal revelation**: Guidance for daily life
- **Comfort**: In times of need
- **Testimony**: Confirmation of truth

### **Specific spiritual gifts**
- Wisdom and knowledge
- Faith to heal and to be healed
- Prophecy and revelation
- Discernment of spirits
- Speaking in tongues and interpretation
- Inspired teaching

## How to receive His influence

### **Personal preparation**
- **Baptism**: By immersion by someone with authority
- **Confirmation**: Receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands
- **Living worthily**: Obeying the commandments
- **Sincere repentance**: Keeping the heart pure

### **Active seeking**
- **Sincere prayer**: Communicating with God in prayer
- **Scripture study**: Reading the scriptures with purpose
- **Service**: Ministering to others with love
- **Obedience**: Following the commandments and the impressions received

## How to recognize His presence

### **Spiritual feelings**
- Deep peace and calm
- Joy and spiritual happiness
- Love toward God and others
- Warmth in the heart

### **Intellectual manifestations**
- Clarity of thought
- Sudden understanding of truths
- Good ideas and inspiration
- Improved memory of spiritual truths

### **Impressions to act**
- Promptings to do good
- Warnings about danger
- Guidance in decisions
- Inspiration to serve

## How to keep His companionship
- **Purity**: Keeping thoughts and actions clean
- **Humility**: Acknowledging dependence on God
- **Obedience**: Following the impressions received
- **Gratitude**: Acknowledging the blessings of God
- **Service**: Loving and serving others

## Consequences of rejecting Him
- Loss of spiritual sensitivity
- Difficulty discerning truth
- Feelings of emptiness and spiritual loneliness
- Greater susceptibility to temptation

The Holy Ghost is the greatest gift a member of the Church can receive after the atoning sacrifice of Jesus Christ, providing constant guidance, comfort, and confirmation of truth throughout life.
