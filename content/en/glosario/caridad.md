# Charity

## Definition
**Charity** is the pure love of Christ. It is the greatest of all spiritual gifts and the supreme attribute that the children of God can develop. It is a selfless, unconditional, and enduring love toward God and toward all His children.

## Characteristics of charity

### **Pure and unconditional love**
- **Without expectations**: It seeks no reward or recognition
- **Universal**: It extends to all people without exception
- **Enduring**: It does not fade with time or circumstances
- **Perfect**: It reflects the love Christ has for us

### **Attributes of charity according to the scriptures**
According to [1 Corinthians 13:4-8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/13?lang=eng&id=p4-p8#p4), charity:
- **Suffereth long**: Patient with the weaknesses of others
- **Is kind**: Gentle and good in every circumstance
- **Envieth not**: Feels no jealousy of others' success
- **Vaunteth not itself**: Does not praise itself
- **Is not puffed up**: Is neither proud nor arrogant
- **Doth not behave itself unseemly**: Always acts appropriately
- **Seeketh not her own**: Is not selfish
- **Is not easily provoked**: Does not become angry easily
- **Thinketh no evil**: Forgives and lets go of offenses
- **Rejoiceth not in iniquity**: Grieves over sin
- **Rejoiceth in the truth**: Celebrates righteousness
- **Beareth all things**: Endures every difficulty
- **Believeth all things**: Trusts in the good in people
- **Hopeth all things**: Keeps hope in others
- **Endureth all things**: Perseveres through trials
- **Never faileth**: It is eternal

## Charity as a divine gift

### **Heavenly origin**
- **Gift of God**: It cannot be developed by human effort alone
- **Fruit of the Spirit**: It is received through the Holy Ghost
- **Gift of the Atonement**: Made possible by the sacrifice of Christ
- **Sincere prayer**: It is obtained by asking the Father in faith

### **How to obtain charity**
1. **Pray in faith**: Ask Heavenly Father with all your heart
2. **Follow Christ**: Imitate His example of perfect love
3. **Serve selflessly**: Minister to others without seeking reward
4. **Study the scriptures**: Learn of the love of God
5. **Repent sincerely**: Purify the heart of selfishness
6. **Obey the commandments**: Live worthy of the gift

## Manifestations of charity

### **In the home**
- **Family patience**: Enduring love toward family members
- **Constant forgiveness**: The capacity to forgive repeated faults
- **Quiet service**: Helping without being asked or recognized
- **Personal sacrifice**: Placing family needs before one's own

### **In the community**
- **Compassion for the needy**: Seeing and responding to the needs of others
- **Inclusion**: Welcoming everyone regardless of their differences
- **Anonymous service**: Helping without seeking credit or recognition
- **Love for enemies**: Blessing those who have hurt us

### **In the Church**
- **Unity**: Working for harmony and the common welfare
- **Support for leaders**: Sustaining them with love and prayer
- **Genuine ministering**: Truly caring for other members
- **Mutual edification**: Helping others grow spiritually

## Scriptures on charity

### **New Testament**
> *"And now abideth faith, hope, charity, these three; but the greatest of these is charity"* ([1 Corinthians 13:13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/13?lang=eng&id=p13#p13))

### **Book of Mormon**
> *"Wherefore, my beloved brethren, pray unto the Father with all the energy of heart, that ye may be filled with this love, which he hath bestowed upon all who are true followers of his Son, Jesus Christ"* ([Moroni 7:48](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/7?lang=eng&id=p48#p48))

> *"Wherefore, my beloved brethren, if ye have not charity, ye are nothing, for charity never faileth. Wherefore, cleave unto charity, which is the greatest of all, for all things must fail"* ([Moroni 7:46](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/moro/7?lang=eng&id=p46#p46))

### **Doctrine and Covenants**
> *"And thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself"* ([Doctrine and Covenants 59:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/59?lang=eng&id=p6#p6))

## Eternal importance of charity

### **Requirement for exaltation**
- **Divine attribute**: An essential characteristic of celestial beings
- **Final judgment**: We will be judged by our love
- **Eternal life**: Necessary in order to dwell with God
- **Perfection**: The final goal of spiritual development

### **Personal transformation**
- **Change of heart**: It transforms our fundamental nature
- **Eternal perspective**: It helps us see as God sees
- **True joy**: A source of lasting happiness
- **Inner peace**: It removes anxiety and fear

## Examples of charity in action

### **The perfect example of Christ**
- **Universal love**: He loved sinners, enemies, and strangers
- **Supreme sacrifice**: He gave His life for all mankind
- **Constant service**: He ministered tirelessly to the needy
- **Infinite forgiveness**: He forgave even those who crucified Him

### **Everyday charity**
- **Small acts**: Simple gestures of kindness and consideration
- **Kind words**: Speaking with love and understanding
- **Time given**: Giving our time to those who need it
- **Shared resources**: Using our blessings to bless others

## Charity and other virtues

### **Relationship with faith**
Charity strengthens, and is strengthened by, faith in Christ

### **Connection with hope**
True love creates hope both in the one who loves and in the one who is loved

### **Foundation of service**
Genuine ministering is born of pure love, not of obligation

## Prayer for charity
Mormon teaches us to pray: **"I pray unto the Father with all the energy of my heart that ye may be filled with this love"**. Charity should be the object of our most sincere and constant prayer.

Charity is the attribute that allows us to become like our Savior and eventually like our Heavenly Father. It is the greatest gift we can develop in this life and the key to eternal happiness.
