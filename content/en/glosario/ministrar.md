# Ministering

## Definition
**Ministering** in the context of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints means serving, caring for, and strengthening others following the example of Jesus Christ. It is a fundamental principle of the **[Gospel](evangelio-de-jesucristo)** that goes beyond formal assignments.

## Characteristics of Christian ministry
- **Selfless service**: Helping without expecting reward
- **Genuine love**: Acting motivated by sincere Christian love
- **Personal attention**: Recognizing individual needs
- **Consistency**: Serving continuously, not sporadically

## Ways to minister
1. **Listen with empathy**: Give genuine attention to concerns
2. **Offer practical help**: Assist with physical and temporal needs
3. **Provide spiritual comfort**: Share Gospel principles
4. **Set an example**: Live in a way that inspires others
5. **Pray for others**: Intercede with the Lord for their welfare

## The Church's ministering program
The Church has implemented a formal ministering program where:
- **Ministering brothers**: Men assigned to care for families
- **Ministering sisters**: Women who minister to other women and families
- **Focus on people**: Prioritize relationships over reports
- **Personal inspiration**: Follow the promptings of the Spirit

## Related scriptures
> *"And whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant"* (Matthew 20:27)

> *"Therefore, strengthen your brethren in all your conversation, in all your prayers, in all your exhortations, and in all your doings"* (Doctrine and Covenants 108:7)

## The Savior's example
Jesus Christ is the perfect example of ministering. He "went about doing good" (Acts 10:38), healing, comforting, and blessing all without distinction.

## Blessings of ministering
- **Personal spiritual growth**: Those who serve draw closer to Christ
- **Strengthened faith**: Service increases one's **[testimony](testimonio)**
- **Unity in community**: Creates strong bonds among **[members](miembro)**
- **Preparation for eternity**: Develops divine attributes
