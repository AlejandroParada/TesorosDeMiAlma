# Primary

**Organization of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**

Primary is the auxiliary organization of the Church for children from 18 months to 11 years of age. Its purpose is to help parents teach their children the gospel of Jesus Christ and prepare each child to make and keep sacred covenants.

## Main characteristics:

- **Classes by age**: Children are organized into classes according to their age
- **Dedicated teachers**: Sisters who teach with love and patience
- **Songs and activities**: Teaching method adapted for children
- **Presentations**: Children participate in special programs
- **Preparation**: Prepares children for baptism at age 8

## Typical activities:

- Sharing time with music and lessons
- Specific classes by age groups
- Memorization of articles of faith
- Songs from the Children's Songbook
- Annual Primary program presentation

Primary helps create a solid foundation of faith and testimony in children's hearts, complementing teaching in the home.
