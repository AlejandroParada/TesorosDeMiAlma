# Member

A **member** of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is a person who has been baptized and confirmed in the Church.

## How to become a member:

- **Baptism by immersion**: Performed by someone who holds priesthood authority
- **Confirmation**: Receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost through the laying on of hands
- **Age**: Generally from age 8 (age of accountability)

## Responsibilities of members:

- Live according to the principles of the Gospel of Jesus Christ
- Participate in Sunday meetings and Church activities
- Serve in callings when extended
- Pay tithes and offerings
- Participate in missionary work
- Perform sacred ordinances in the temple

## Blessings of membership:

- Constant companionship of the Holy Ghost
- Access to all ordinances of salvation
- Belonging to God's family
- Guidance through living prophets and apostles
- Hope of eternal life with family

Members strive to follow the example of Jesus Christ and help build His kingdom on earth.
