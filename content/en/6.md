# 6 The Story of the Home I Visited as a Young Man

*"Then saith he to the disciple, Behold thy mother! And from that hour that disciple took her unto his own home."* - [John 19:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/19?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

I remember that when I was a young man I was assigned an older sister to visit, together with my companion, in what was then called "Home Teaching." The sister's name was Angélica, and I don't think there is a name more fitting than that for her. In fact, it was my first assignment to visit with a companion, which filled me with eagerness and nerves, a whole intense mix of conflicting emotions.

The sister was very happy to know we would be coming to visit her. We introduced ourselves at the chapel on Sunday to set a day to visit her, and we also asked for her address. She told us she was delighted that we would be her "home teachers," and that she would be waiting for us with her sisters at her house. She asked us to be on time, for it would be the following Wednesday at four in the afternoon.

The day of the visit came, and we went to the address she had given us, which was along Avenida Millán. When we reached the address, we could see in the distance a house set back from the street, surrounded by a large lot with a lawn in front.

At first we doubted the address had been written correctly, because as we drew closer we saw that it was a home for the elderly. We looked carefully, and several people were already sitting outside, enjoying the beautiful day. Among them we recognized several ladies, including Sister Angélica.

We drew near, and Sister Angélica warmly introduced us to her "sisters." They were all radiant and happy to see us. There was an older lady, the eldest of them all, of whom Angélica said to us: "She is like my mom." They were all quite lucid and alert.

I truly do not remember ever seeing older women so genuinely happy and content. It was plain that Angélica was that special bond, an essential part of their everyday happiness.

Angélica would say to them tenderly: "This is what they are like. Look at them closely, because when we are on the other side they will be just the same, and you must listen to their message. They are priests of God."

Then we shared a brief spiritual message, and they were all very happy and grateful for our visit.

On another occasion, Angélica told us something at the chapel that marked me deeply. She explained that the ladies at the home had said to her sadly: "Our children and our grandchildren don't come to visit us anymore."

And Angélica, her heart full of love, had answered each of them: "Don't worry. From now on you are my sister. You have me. I am your sister now, and I will love you as my own sister." She spoke these comforting words to each one, assuring them they need not worry, because they would not be alone: they were with their adoptive sister.

But what moved me most was what she said to the eldest lady: "You will be my mother, and I will be your daughter."

This simple yet profound act of love changed the lives of those women forever. They radiated light, love, and happiness because someone had forgotten herself in order to give unconditional love to others.

Christ promised: *"I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you"* ([John 14:18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?lang=eng&id=p18#p18)), and when He was on the cross, He said to His disciple: *"Behold thy mother"*, charging him to care for His mother as if she were his own.

I have seen many acts of love throughout my life, but few as special as this one. Angélica was someone who, without abundant material means, with only her strength and her great heart, forgot herself completely in order to give pure love and selfless care to her neighbor.

This, truly, is a treasure for my soul: to see how a person can transform the lives of others simply by choosing to love without conditions, creating a family of the heart where before there had been loneliness.
