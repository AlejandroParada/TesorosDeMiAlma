# 30 The Story of Peter Church

> **"For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them."** ([Matthew 18:20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p20#p20))

> **"And the strangers that join themselves to the Lord, to serve him, and that love the name of the Lord, to be his servants... them will I bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer."** ([Isaiah 56:6-7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/56?lang=eng&id=p6-p7#p6))

## An extraordinary encounter

Peter Church was an extraordinary man of 80, full of vitality and with an energy that defied his age. I met him in the Ciudad de la Costa Ward, where we both attended Church meetings.

Because I spoke English, he was immensely happy when he learned that he could talk with me in his native language. He was British by birth, and after Church meetings we used to walk together toward his house. That walk was always full of fascinating stories that Peter had lived through over his eight decades of life.

## An extraordinary athletic past

In his youth, Peter had been part of England's Olympic track and field delegation for the Olympics held just before the Second World War. Despite his advanced age, he kept an impressive athletic condition. Walking more than 2 kilometers each way from his house to the chapel was no effort for him. At other times he made the trip on an old bicycle that he cared for with great attention.

## Family roots in Uruguay

His father had been a British cultural attaché, and by the circumstances of fate he ended up settling permanently in Uruguay with his whole family. That is how Peter, while still young, came to these South American lands that would eventually become his true home.

## The call of duty

Peter was called to military service for his native country and to take part in the Second World War. During his journey toward Europe to join the battlefront, however, they received word that the war had ended. With relief and gratitude, he returned to Uruguay, where he was able to take up his life again in peace.

## A life dedicated to nature

He worked professionally in botany, and his passion for plants was evident in every conversation. At his house he had a small park and an artificial pond where he cultivated, with love and patience, various plant species. He loved Uruguay deeply because he had found peace and quiet here, and because it had truly become his home and his refuge.

## Simplicity as a philosophy of life

Peter lived a simple, austere life, without unnecessary complications. He had learned to value simple things: a good conversation, the growth of a plant, a walk under the Uruguayan sun, and especially the company of friends who shared his spiritual values.

## A silent testimony

What impressed me most about Peter was his silent but powerful testimony. He never preached openly, but his whole life was a demonstration of the gospel of Jesus Christ. His kindness, his perseverance in walking kilometers to Church, his love for God's creation, and his grateful spirit were living scripture.

## A friendship that transcends barriers

In me, Peter found more than a walking companion; he found a friend with whom to talk about life, the gospel, and ways of overcoming the obstacles that existence presents. Despite our different nationalities and generations, we shared the common faith that united us as brothers.

## Lessons in perseverance

Peter taught me that age is not a barrier to serving the Lord. His faithfulness in attending meetings, regardless of distance or weather, was a constant example of spiritual dedication. Every Sunday, there was Peter, with his kind smile and his heart ready to worship.

## Faith that finds a home

Peter's story is also the story of how the gospel can find a home in any heart, regardless of origin, nationality, or circumstances. He had traveled from the British Isles to the shores of the Río de la Plata, and in both places he had found the same truth: that God loves all His children.

## A legacy of simplicity and love

Peter Church taught me that a full life is not measured by outward achievements or material possessions, but by the ability to find joy in simple things, to love God's creation, and to keep the faith through all the seasons of life.

## A prayer of gratitude

When I remember Peter, my heart fills with gratitude for having had the blessing of knowing a man who lived the gospel with such authenticity and simplicity. His example reminded me that true greatness is found in daily faithfulness, in love for nature, and in the willingness to walk—literally and figuratively—toward the house of the Lord.

## The treasure of friendship

Peter's friendship became one of the most precious treasures for my soul. He taught me that the best conversations happen during walks, that wisdom comes with experience, and that a grateful heart can turn any place into a home.

Peter Church: Olympic athlete, botanist, war veteran, but above all a faithful disciple of Jesus Christ who found in Uruguay not only a refuge, but a place where his testimony could flourish like the plants he loved so much.
