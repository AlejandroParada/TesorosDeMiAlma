10 The School Snack

"It is more blessed to give than to receive." (Acts 20:35)

When I was in school, I remember that always or almost every day I carried cookies in a carefully wrapped package, where my mother put two or three cookies for us. My father worked as a pastry chef in a bakery, and every day he brought cookies that they gave him at the end of the workday. The bakery owner would tell them, "What's left is for you."

One day in the first days of class, during school recess, there was a group of classmates (about four or five) who were paying close attention to my snack, and I saw that they didn't bring anything for their snack. So I decided to divide my snack so they could also eat some cookies. We divided the cookies in the best way possible, and every day I shared my cookies with Juan, Miguel, Carlitos, José, and Marcos.

The weekend arrived, and when talking with my parents after chapel meetings, I told them what was happening during recess. They listened attentively. I thought it hadn't mattered to them, so I said to myself, "Well, I think I'm doing good—they don't have anything for their snack." The week began, Monday passed, and... Tuesday arrived. In the morning as we got ready to go to school, I saw several carefully prepared packages on the table, and I saw that my mother had written something on each package—a name. When she was going to give me the snack to put in my backpack, she said, taking the first package, "Give this one to Juan," and I put it in my backpack. "Give this other one to Miguel," "Give this other one to Carlitos," "Give this other one to José and this one to Marcos." When I arrived at school and recess time came, I took out my snack. Under the attentive gaze of my classmates, I said, "Juan, I have something for you..." and I gave him one of the packages. Then I did the same with Miguel, Carlitos, José, and Marcos. You don't know the joy that lit up their faces—that day I felt like I was eating the most delicious cookies in the world!

How grateful I am to have had an attentive mother who gave the best she knew how, like every good mother does.
