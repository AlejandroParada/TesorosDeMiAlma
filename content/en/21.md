# 21 My Daughter’s Prayer

*“And they did rejoice with exceeding great joy… and also all their children that could speak lifted their voices in the praises of their God.”* — [Mosiah 24:21–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/24?lang=eng&id=p21-p22#p21)

One night, while my wife was teaching our daughter how to say the family prayer before we went to bed, something happened that we will never forget. My daughter, who at that time was only 4 or 5 years old, was kneeling beside us but was a little out of sorts and whining. I think she simply was sleepy after a long day of play and activities.

## The Lesson of Gratitude

Her mother, with the characteristic patience that only mothers possess, was carefully teaching her the most important part of any prayer: giving thanks to our Heavenly Father.

“Now say: ‘We thank Thee…,’” she explained tenderly, “and think of everything God has given you and thank Him for each blessing.”

My little daughter began to reflect in silence, as only children know how to do when they take something seriously. But then, suddenly, she started to cry again, this time more intensely.

Worried, we asked her: “Why are you crying now, my love?”

## The Answer That Moved Us

With the pure and innocent honesty that only children have, through tears, she answered:

“Because now I won’t be able to sleep all night!”

We stood completely frozen and astonished for a few moments. We could not believe what we had just heard.

## The Awakening of Awareness

In that moment we understood the depth of what had happened. Our little daughter had begun to truly reflect on all the blessings God had given her, and she had realized that there were so many, so many, that she would need the whole night to thank Him for them one by one.

Her crying was not of sadness or tiredness, but of overwhelming gratitude. It was the cry of a pure heart that had recognized the immensity of God’s love in her little life.

## The Lesson for Adults

There we were, two adults, completely humbled by the spiritual wisdom of a five-year-old girl. We realized that sometimes we adults do not even stop to recognize a fraction of what God has given us.

How much more have we as adults received from the generous hands of our Heavenly Father, and how few times do we truly stop to thank Him with the sincerity of a heart that is truly aware?

## The Scriptures Come to Life

That night, the words of Mosiah took on a completely new meaning in our home: *“And also all their children that could speak lifted their voices in the praises of their God.”*

Children, with their purity of heart, often see and feel what we adults have lost in our daily routine. They instinctively recognize the hand of God in simple things: a sunny day, a warm meal, a comfortable bed, the love of mom and dad, a favorite toy, a sibling’s laughter.

## The Treasure of Innocence

My daughter taught me that night that true gratitude is not a quick list of “thanks for this and for that.” True gratitude is a deep and overwhelming recognition that everything—absolutely everything good in our lives—comes from a Heavenly Father who loves us infinitely.

That prayer interrupted by tears of gratitude became one of the most precious treasures of my soul. It reminded me that we must draw near to God with the hearts of children: pure, sincere, and completely open to recognizing His goodness in our lives.

And that perhaps, if we truly reflected on all the blessings we have received, we too would stay awake all night—not from worry, but from pure and overwhelming gratitude.

Sometimes it is the children who teach us the deepest lessons about faith, gratitude, and the love of God.
