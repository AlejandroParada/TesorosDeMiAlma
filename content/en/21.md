# 21 My Daughter's Prayer

"...they did pour out their thanks to God... and all their children who could speak did raise their voices in praise unto their God." (Mosiah 24:21-22)

On one occasion when my wife was teaching my daughter, who was very small at the time—4 or 5 years old—to pray before bedtime while kneeling, she was a little grumpy and somewhat crying, I think because she was sleepy. Her mother was patiently teaching her the thanksgiving part of the prayer, telling her, "Now say 'we thank thee...' and think of everything God has given you and thank him for it." Then she started crying again, and "Why are you crying now?" And she responded, "Because now I won't be able to sleep all night!" There we stood frozen and amazed that our daughter recognized everything God had given her, and that sometimes we don't even realize—how much more as adults have we received from God and don't thank Him for it!
